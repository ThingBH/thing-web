# Social lane

Posts (image + caption) and reels (short video). Not the website: none of `docs/engineering.md` (Astro, islands, prefetch, Daylight look) applies here.

**Writing any post or reel copy:**
- `../docs/brand/claims.md` what you may and may not claim. Every post traces to the real product.
- `../docs/brand/voice.md` CTA (free trial, `thing.bh/free-trial`), English and Arabic never mixed in one post, Gym Thing stays Latin, support.
- `../docs/brand/product.md` what the product does and how to depict it (ABC Gym, rebuilt HTML, never screenshots).

## Skills

- **Reels / video:** `hyperframes` (read first), then `hyperframes-*`, `media-use`, `reels-scripting`.
- **Posts:** write the caption and brief directly; reuse `blog-post` research when a post draws on a published article.
- Website skills (`design-taste-frontend`, `emil-design-eng`, `animate*`) are for the site, not social.

## Layout

Flat lane. One folder per deliverable, named by date + topic (`2026-10-member-app-launch/`). A reel folder is a HyperFrames project. `.gitignore` drops renders, `.media`, `.hyperframes`, `node_modules`; only source (scripts, captions, briefs) is committed.
