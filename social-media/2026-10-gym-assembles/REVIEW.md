# Review: draft 1

- **Built:** the owner's concept from SCRIPT.md. No alternative storylines were pitched.
- **Creative calls:** an architectural white-model look; the dashboard stays flat to camera (product UI never tilts); the door slides open on the dive so the cut lands on the app's own canvas colour (#f8f9fb).
- **Measured:** `check` passes with no errors. Audio is -14 LUFS with a -3 dB true peak, and the hits land at 0.3, 2.46, 3.85, 5.25, 6.66, 10.21, 13.4 and 20.05 s. The sound was measured, not heard.
- **Sound:** sound effects only, from the bundled offline set. There is no music bed because HeyGen isn't installed. To add music: install the `heygen` CLI and run `heygen auth login --oauth`, or drop an MP3 into `assets/`.
- **Weakest shot:** 10.1 to 10.5 s, about 0.35 s of plain white before the dashboard appears. The slab's top also reads nearly as white as the blocks.
- **Warnings kept:** `timeline_track_too_dense` (five word clips on one lane, which only affects readability in Studio) and one contrast warning on a word mid-fade from grey to ink.
