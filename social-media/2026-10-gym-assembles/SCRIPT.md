# Reel: The gym assembles

**One thing to remember:** everything your gym runs on lives in one place.

**Format:** 1080x1920, 30 fps, about 22 s. HyperFrames + Three.js for the 3D building. English cut first; Arabic is a separate cut.
**Sound:** music bed, a thud on each module landing, a whoosh on the dive, a soft click as the screen locks, a short sting on the logo. No voiceover. Titles carry the words.
**Look:** light canvas, black ink, Geist type, soft contact shadows. Modules are matte white blocks. Product colours appear only inside the dashboard.
**Safe band:** all text and UI between 168 px and 1330 px from the top.

## UI pieces in scene 4 (real labels from the product)

- **Doors** (Access control): an access log row: Time, Member, Door: Reception, Result: **Allowed**.
- **Payments**: a Point of Sale total: Subtotal, Total.
- **Members**: the Customers list: Name, Plan, Status, Ends.
- **Payroll** (HR and payroll): a payslip line: Gross pay.
- **Dashboard they lock into**: "Today at a Glance" with Classes, New Customers, Revenue. Sidebar: Dashboard, Customers, Classes, Point of Sale, Payroll.

All numbers are sample data for ABC Gym. No real customer figures.

## Rules this reel follows

- Product UI never tilts in 3D. The blocks are 3D; the dashboard is flat and faces the camera.
- No dark void with floating cards, no glow or particles, no three cards in a row.
- Logo lands once, on the end card, never docked in a corner.
- Claims checked against `docs/brand/claims.md`: all four modules are built.
