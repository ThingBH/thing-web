## 2. Meta's WhatsApp Business rules a gym should know

### WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy (last updated September 23, 2026)
URL: https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/ (business.whatsapp.com/policy redirects here). The page says "Last updated: September 23, 2026".

Opt-in:
- "You may only contact people on WhatsApp if: (a) they have given you their mobile phone number or username; and (b) you have received opt-in permission from the recipient confirming that they wish to receive subsequent messages or calls from you."
- "You are solely responsible for determining the method of opt-in, that you have obtained opt-in in a manner that complies with laws applicable to your communications, and that you have otherwise provided notices and obtained permissions that are required under applicable law."

Opt-out:
- "You must respect all requests (either on or off WhatsApp) by a person to block, discontinue, or otherwise opt out of communications from you via WhatsApp, including removing that person from your contacts list. Do not confuse, deceive, defraud, mislead, spam, or surprise people with your communications."

Templates and the 24-hour window:
- "You may only initiate conversations using an approved Message Template (as defined in our documentation)."
- "You may reply to a user message without use of a Message Template as long as it’s within the 24-hour customer service window, which opens and resets with each user message. Outside the 24-hour customer service window, you may only send messages via approved Message Templates."

Automation and handover to a person:
- "You may use automation when responding during the 24-hour window, but must also have available prompt, clear, and direct escalation paths." The listed paths include "In-Chat Human Agent transfer", "Phone number", "Email", "In-store visits (ex: visit retail store, bank branch)".

Best practices for opt-in:
- "Users should expect the messages and calls they receive."
- "Obtaining an opt-in that encompasses the different categories of messages that you will send (ex: order updates, relevant offers, product recommendations, etc.)"
- "Provide clear instructions for how people can opt out of receiving specific categories of messages or calls, and honor these requests."

### WhatsApp Business Platform pricing (Meta developer docs)
URL: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing (developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing redirects here). The page has no "updated" date. It says it "reflects pricing updates that became effective as of October 1, 2026 by Messaging account timezone."
- "Template messages ("type":"template") can be delivered at any time."
- Template categories: "Marketing – template messages for promotional objectives", "Utility – template messages for transactional or informational objectives", "Authentication – template messages for identity verification objectives".
- "Businesses can use non-template messages only to respond to users – Non-template messages ("type":"text", "type":"image", and so on) can only be sent in an open 24-hour customer service window, which opens and resets with each user message. Note that a business can also choose to use template messages to respond to users; however, non-template messages cannot be used to reach out to users."
- "Effective July 1, 2025 – Meta charges on a per-message basis, aligned to industry standards. Meta charges by category–market, and only for messages from businesses to users. Meta does not charge for messages from users to businesses."
- From the "Effective October 1, 2026" section: "Meta resumed charging, on a per-message basis, for messages that were previously free". It also says utility messages sent in an open customer service window are charged again from that date.
- "For each business phone number, Meta provides 1,000 free service messages per month, which do not roll over but reset monthly."
- Gulf note: the page lists "Rest of Middle East – 4 markets", with Bahrain (BH) - 973 in that group. Saudi Arabia and the UAE have their own rate-card rows. It also says: "Until September 30, 2026, messages to users in these markets were charged the respective regional rates (for example, Rest of Middle East for Kuwait)."

## 3. Gulf gym members and WhatsApp

### App Store reviews (reviewers not named)
Both Saudi reviews below came from Apple's own reviews feed, itunes.apple.com/{country}/rss/customerreviews. The App Store web page only shows a curated set of reviews, and these two aren't in it. The GymNation review does appear on the web page.

1. **Fitness Time | وقت اللياقة** (Leejam Sports Company), Saudi App Store, 1 star, 11 June 2026, app version 1.1.38. Title: "مخادعين"
   "حصلت على عرض خصم وعند التجديد وجدته لم يتم تفعيله … والشكاوي لا تعمل ولا الواتساب يعمل … تجربة سيئة والقادم ان شاء الله مع أندية اخرى."

3. **GymNation** (GymNation), UAE App Store, 1 star, 22 Sept 2026. Title: "No option to cancel the subscription even after 12 months (End of contract)"
   "There is proper way to cancel it. It’s been more than 13 months and they still charge every month. And there is contact info provided to send the email that my contract has ended.    Shown on https://apps.apple.com/ae/app/gymnation/id6759922886?see-all=reviews

Pattern: all three members went to WhatsApp for a renewal, sign-up or cancellation, and all three complained that nobody replied there. Members expect the gym to be on WhatsApp, and they hold it against the gym when no one answers.

I also checked recent App Store reviews (up to 500 per app) for B_FIT, OPTIMO (Armah), PureGym Arabia, PT PureGym KSA, Fitness First MENA (UAE, Saudi, Bahrain), Fitness Time (UAE) and the older GymNation app (id1669518396). None of them mention WhatsApp.
