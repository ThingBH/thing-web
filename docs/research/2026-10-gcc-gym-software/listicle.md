# GCC gym software listicle: vendor research

Research for "Best gym management software in the GCC (2026)". Every quote below was read on the vendor's own page on **22 Oct 2026** in a real Chrome (headless, CDP), copied from the page text. Quotes are verbatim in double quotes. Text in [square brackets] is my note or an English gloss of Arabic, not a quote. "Date" is the date the page shows; where a page shows only a footer copyright year, that is given, otherwise "read 22 Oct 2026".

**Pages I could not open:** none in the final set. `flybyglobal.com` returned a Cloudflare challenge to WebFetch (HTTP 403) and to plain headless Chrome. It opened in Chrome once a normal desktop user agent was set, so the FLYBY quotes are from the real page. Pages that opened but had **no prices**: `glofox.com/pricing/` (a quote form only), `tamarran.com/en/business` (a "Pricing" nav link, but no prices on the page), `etopme.ae/gym-management-software-uae/` ("Get Quote !" form). I did not use the vendor's own blog posts, or third-party directories (Capterra, GetApp, startup-seeker), as sources.

Caveats worth knowing before writing:
- Prices shown are what my browser was served. Daftra showed USD, and Easy Gym showed INR (₹), so GCC visitors may see other currencies.
- Rekaz and Memvera show prices with the new Saudi riyal symbol (an icon, not text). I confirmed it from screenshots of the pricing cards.
- Several pages carry testimonials and stats. I recorded them only where they matter, and none of them are independently verified.
