## 3. BenefitPay volumes, H1 2026

(Same text on TradeArabia, 1 Sep 2026: https://tradearabia.com/News/467411/BENEFIT-s-transaction-value-for-H1-surges-6.1pc-to-%2451.2bn)

> BENEFIT, a leading innovator in Bahrain and a major player in the fintech and electronic financial transactions service, has announced that it had processed 261.2 million transactions through the Electronic Fund Transfer System (EFTS) in the first half of 2026, up 7.6% from a year earlier.
> 
> The total value of these transactions surged by 6.1% to hit BD19.3 billion ($51.2 billion). The transactions, which were processed through bank channels and the BenefitPay application, included only successfully completed transactions.
> 
> The transaction volume increased from 242.7 million in the first half while transaction value rose from BD18.2 billion, reflecting continued growth in digital payments and transfers, said Bahrain's top electronic payments network in its statement.
> 
> BenefitPay accounted for about 246 million EFTS transactions worth BD5.4 billion, up 7.2% in both volume and value from the same period a year earlier, when it processed 229.4 million transactions worth BD5.1 billion.
