# Claims: what we may and may not say

Copy says what the owner gets, in plain words, never how the system works inside. Every claim traces to code in the-gym-thing (see `product.md`):

- **Built** (including built but switched off, e.g. Apple Wallet, member class booking, WhatsApp in automations): say it as available today. We never say "coming soon" or "rolling out".
- **No trace in code**: leave it off and tell the owner (e.g. CPR card at the door, buying plans in the member app, credit notes, Arabic invoices).
- **Untrue, always off**: "hosted in Bahrain" (it's AWS Paris), ZATCA, uptime SLA, fake customers, testimonials or numbers, anything unsourced about a competitor.

Nameable clients: Train Fitness, RSX Fitness, Pulse Fitness, Core District Gym, Elevation Fitness Club.

Competitor facts live in `src/data/compare.ts`, each with a source and checked date; update both together.
