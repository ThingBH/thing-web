# Voice & language

- **One call to action everywhere: Start with a free trial** (`/free-trial/`). There is no quote flow and no pricing page; price is "one yearly price by gym size, everything included".
- **English and Arabic never mix in one piece of copy.** On the site they are separate routes (`/`, `/ar/`). "Gym Thing" stays in Latin script in Arabic. Arabic copy is light Bahraini dialect; page titles and meta descriptions are formal Arabic. Legal pages (privacy, terms, data deletion) are English only. Product screens stay in English on both languages, as the product is.
- **Support is a headline value:** WhatsApp, phone, email, 7 days a week, same-day replies.
