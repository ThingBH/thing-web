# GCC pages plan (Oct 2026)

Goal: rank for "gym software <country>" across the GCC the way thing.bh already ranks for "gym software in bahrain", in English and Arabic.

These are marketing pages. The module pages already describe every feature, so the country pages sell Gym Thing to gyms in each country and link to the modules. Copy stays positive and local. It does not name anything the system lacks (ZATCA, KNET, mada and so on), per the claims rules in CLAUDE.md.

Research: `~/Documents/Last30Days/gym-management-software-gcc-bahrain-saudi-arabia-uae-qatar-kuwait-oman-raw-v3.md` (22 Oct 2026).

## Why this can win

- **English results.** One vendor, Easy Gym Software, has a page per country (`/saudi-arabia`, `/uae`, `/qatar`, `/bahrain`), and they are thin. Everything else is directories and agency pages.
- **Arabic results.** There are almost no real vendor pages, only freelancer gigs and classified ads. Arabic is the biggest opening.
- **Oman and Kuwait.** No vendor has a page for either.

## Pages

Every page is built in English and Arabic from data, like the modules and comparisons, with the share card, JSON-LD, the Markdown copy and the `llms.txt` entry generated by the build.

1. **GCC hub, `/gym-software/`.**
   - Title: "Gym management software for the GCC".
   - A short pitch, the module showcase, and a link card for each country.
2. **Six country pages, `/gym-software/<country>/`:** `bahrain`, `saudi-arabia`, `uae`, `qatar`, `kuwait` and `oman`.
   - **Hero:** an H1 such as "Gym management software in Saudi Arabia" and a local lede.
   - **Local section:** written for that country, so the pages aren't near-copies. Google treats near-identical regional pages as doorway pages. Each one names:
     - its cities (Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam; Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah; and so on);
     - its VAT rate (Gym Thing applies your rate);
     - its weekend and Gulf hours, for support;
     - the Arabic member app;
     - WhatsApp payment links.
   - **Modules:** the existing module showcase with "Read more" links, which covers every feature.
   - **FAQ:** questions specific to the country, with FAQPage JSON-LD.
   - **Call to action:** "Start with a free trial".
   - **JSON-LD:** `areaServed` set to the country on the SoftwareApplication, plus breadcrumbs.
3. **Footer links** to the hub and all six countries on every page ("Gym software in Bahrain · Saudi Arabia · UAE · …"). This is internal linking from every page.
4. **Homepage:** the title becomes "for Bahrain and the GCC".

### Arabic copy

Country H1s and titles use the words people search for: "برنامج إدارة النوادي الرياضية في السعودية", "نظام إدارة الجيم في الإمارات", "برنامج إدارة صالات رياضية". Titles and meta descriptions are in formal Arabic, and page copy is in light dialect, per the site rules.

## Blog posts (blog-post skill, English and Arabic)

1. "Best gym management software in the GCC (2026)", a listicle that includes Gym Thing. Listicles rank: Easy Gym's Dubai listicle does. Competitor facts are sourced and dated, as in `compare.ts`.
2. "Opening a gym in Saudi Arabia / UAE: the software checklist."
3. "VAT on gym memberships in each GCC country (2026)", a sourced rate table: KSA 15%, Bahrain 10%, UAE and Oman 5%, Qatar and Kuwait none.
4. "How to run a gym with WhatsApp in the Gulf": payment links, renewals and reminders.

## Off-site (no code)

Get Gym Thing listed on Capterra AE / GetApp, SoftwareSuggest UAE, Krowdbase and Zoftwarehub. These directories rank for every GCC country.

## Code shape

- `src/data/countries.ts`, with `countries.ar.ts` keyed by slug. The build fails if the two fall out of step, as with `modules.ar.ts`.
- `src/views/Countries.astro` (the hub) and `src/views/Country.astro`.
- Routes: `src/pages/gym-software/index.astro` and `[slug].astro` (+ `[slug].md.ts`), mirrored under `src/pages/ar/`.
- Footer links, `llms.txt` and the sitemap are generated from `countries.ts`.

## Later

- City pages (Riyadh, Jeddah, Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Doha, Muscat, Kuwait City), once Search Console shows impressions for them.
- The `.bh` domain tells Google the site is for Bahrain, which limits how high it can rank in other countries. Revisit a generic domain if the country pages stall.

## Measure

Search Console → Performance, filtered by country (SA, AE, QA, KW, OM) and query. Check rankings monthly for "gym software <country>", "gym management software <city>" and the Arabic equivalents.
