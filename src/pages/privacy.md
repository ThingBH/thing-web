# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 12 Oct 2026

Thing Company ("Thing Company", "we", "us", or "our") is a digital platform operated by **Thing W.L.L**, Commercial Registration No. **196454-1**, headquartered in **Seef, Kingdom of Bahrain**.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect personal data when you use the Thing Company platform and related services (collectively, the "Platform"). This policy is issued in accordance with Bahrain's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 30 of 2018) ("PDPL").

By accessing or using Thing Company, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

## Apps covered by this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the following apps, each published on Google Play by the developer **Thing W.L.L.** and on the Apple App Store by **Thing W.L.L**, in addition to the Thing Company marketplace platform and the thing.bh website:

- **Gym Thing** (Android package / iOS bundle identifier **bh.thing.gym**) – the **member app** of the Gym Thing platform, used by members (customers) of gyms that subscribe to the platform (see section 9).
- **Gym Thing Admin** (Android package / iOS bundle identifier **bh.thing.gym.admin**) – the **staff app** of the Gym Thing platform, used by employees and managers of gyms that subscribe to the platform (see section 8).
- **Train Fitness** (Android package / iOS bundle identifier **bh.thing.trainfitness**) – the **member app** of the Train Fitness gym, built on the same software as the Gym Thing member app and covered by the same section 9.

Gym Thing and Gym Thing Admin are two separate apps with different users and different data practices: "Gym Thing" (bh.thing.gym) is for gym members, and "Gym Thing Admin" (bh.thing.gym.admin) is for gym staff. In this policy, "the Gym Thing platform" means the gym-management service behind these apps, "the member apps" means Gym Thing and Train Fitness, and "the staff app" means Gym Thing Admin.

Other apps we may publish for the Gym Thing platform are also covered by this policy.

**Which sections apply to you.** Sections 3 to 7 and section 11 (Personal Data We Collect, Purpose of Processing, Payments & Escrow, Third-Party Service Providers, Data Storage & Localization, and Marketing Communications) describe the **Thing Company marketplace and the thing.bh website only**. They do not describe the Gym Thing platform apps. For the staff app, section 8 applies **instead of** those sections, and for the member apps, section 9 applies **instead of** those sections – not in addition to them. What Gym Thing, Train Fitness and Gym Thing Admin collect, use and share is set out only in sections 8 and 9. Sections 1, 2, 10 and 12 to 15 apply to all our services.

## 8. Staff App (Gym Thing Admin)

This section applies specifically to **Gym Thing Admin** (package/bundle id **bh.thing.gym.admin**), the staff app used by employees and managers of gyms subscribed to the Gym Thing platform. For this app it applies instead of sections 3 to 7 and 11, which describe the Thing Company marketplace only. It does not apply to the member app "Gym Thing" (bh.thing.gym), which is covered by section 9.

### 8.1 Data We Collect

- **Account identity** – email address and password/authentication tokens (via AWS Cognito), your gym/organisation association, and your role and permissions
- **Employee profile data** provided by your employer gym – name, employee number, job title/department, contact details, and HR records the gym maintains, including attendance, shifts, leave, and, for gyms using payroll, salary/payslip data the employer records
- **Attendance data** – check-in/check-out timestamps and status
- **Location data** – see "Location Data" below
- **Device and technical data** – device type, OS version, app version, and basic diagnostic logs

### 8.2 Location Data

Gym Thing Admin captures a single location fix (latitude, longitude, and accuracy) only at the moment an employee checks in or out, only while the app is in use in the foreground ("when in use" permission), used solely to verify that the check-in happened at the assigned branch (geofence verification). This location fix is stored with the attendance record. The app does not track location continuously or in the background, and location data is never sold or used for advertising.

### 8.3 Why We Collect This Data

We use this data to provide the staff app (attendance, scheduling, member check-in, and payroll access where enabled by the employer gym), to verify attendance location, for security and fraud prevention, to support the employer gym's HR and compliance obligations, and to provide customer support.

### 8.4 Sharing & Processors

- **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** – hosting, authentication, storage, and email/SMS/push delivery. Data for the Gym Thing platform is hosted in the AWS **eu-west-3 (Paris, European Union)** region
- **Your employer gym** – your employer gym is the data controller for your HR data; Thing W.L.L processes it on the gym's behalf

We do not sell personal data collected through Gym Thing Admin, and we do not share it with advertising networks.

### 8.5 App Permissions

- **Location ("when in use")** – to verify check-in/check-out at the assigned branch, as described above
- **Camera** – to scan member QR codes at reception, requested only when the employee opens the scanner; the app does not read your photos
- **Notifications** – to deliver work notifications; the app does not read your contacts

Gym Thing Admin does not use any third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

### 8.6 Retention & Deletion

We retain this data for as long as your account or employment record with your employer gym is active, and as required by the employer's legal and payroll record-keeping obligations. To request deletion, contact [hello@thing.bh](mailto:hello@thing.bh) or see our [Account & Data Deletion](/data-deletion/) page.

## 9. Member Apps (Gym Thing and Train Fitness)

This section applies specifically to **Gym Thing** (package/bundle id **bh.thing.gym**) and to **Train Fitness** (package/bundle id **bh.thing.trainfitness**), the member apps used by members of gyms subscribed to the Gym Thing platform. For these apps it applies instead of sections 3 to 7 and 11, which describe the Thing Company marketplace only. It does not apply to the staff app "Gym Thing Admin" (bh.thing.gym.admin), which is covered by section 8. Both member apps are published on Google Play by the developer "Thing W.L.L." and on the Apple App Store by "Thing W.L.L". Member accounts are created by your gym; you activate yours in the app with a code emailed to you and then sign in with your email address and password.

### 9.1 Data We Collect

- **Account identity** – email address and your password/authentication tokens, and your gym/organisation association
- **Profile and membership data** held by your gym – name, phone number, membership/customer number, membership status and dates, and the service or personal-training entitlements on your account
- **Access credential data** – the short-lived tokens used to generate the rotating QR code you show at the gym door
- **Class bookings** – the class bookings made for you at your gym, which the app displays. Classes are booked and cancelled at your gym's reception, not in the app
- **Workout data** – see "Fitness and Workout Data" below
- **Technical data** – your IP address and the app and device information sent with requests to our servers, and basic diagnostic logs on the server

### 9.2 Fitness and Workout Data

The app includes a workout tracker. Data you enter in it – exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, workout templates, workout sessions and personal records – is sent to our servers and stored with your account so it is available on your account. Body-weight entries you log are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage, and are not sent to us. We do not read health data from Apple Health, Health Connect or any other health or fitness platform, and we do not collect data from wearables or device sensors.

### 9.3 Why We Collect This Data

We use this data to run your account and sign you in, to show your membership status and entitlements, to let you open the gym door with your QR credential, to show you the class schedule and your class bookings, to provide the workout tracker, for security and fraud prevention, and to provide customer support. We do not use it for advertising and we do not build advertising profiles.

### 9.4 Sharing & Processors

- **Your gym** – your gym is the data controller for your membership data; Thing W.L.L processes it on the gym's behalf as the operator of the Gym Thing platform, and your gym can see the data held on your member account
- **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** – hosting, authentication, storage, and email delivery (for example your activation code). Data for the Gym Thing platform is hosted in the AWS **eu-west-3 (Paris, European Union)** region
- **Google Wallet** – only if you tap "Add to Google Wallet" (offered on Android, for gyms that have enabled it). A pass containing your name and member identifier is then created with Google, and Google's own privacy policy applies to the pass once it is in your Google Wallet
- **Expo (EAS Update)** – the app checks Expo's update service for app updates when it starts, and Expo receives technical request data such as the app version, the platform (iOS or Android) and an anonymous installation identifier; no account or workout data is sent to Expo for this purpose
- **Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging)** – on Android, the app includes this Google service so that it can receive notifications. It assigns an app-installation identifier on your device and registers it with Google when the app starts. On iOS, the Apple Push Notification service is used only if you allow notifications. No notification token is sent to our servers unless you allow notifications and the gym platform enables them

We do not sell personal data collected through the member apps, and we do not share it with advertising networks.

### 9.5 App Permissions

- **Notifications (optional)** – the app may ask for your permission to send notifications. Permission is optional: nothing is sent until you allow notifications and the gym platform enables them. Notifications are used only for notices about your gym, your membership and your classes, and can be turned off at any time in your device's system settings. The app works without them

The member apps do not request location, camera, contacts, photos/media library, microphone, Bluetooth or health-data permissions. They do not track your location, do not scan or read your photos, and do not read your contacts. The apps require network access to communicate with our servers. They contain no third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.

### 9.6 Data Stored on Your Device

Current versions of the app store your sign-in tokens in your device's secure storage (the iOS Keychain, or Android Keystore-backed encrypted storage), falling back to the app's private storage only if secure storage is unavailable on the device; older versions stored them in the app's private storage. Basic account details, your language setting, and your body-weight entries and locally cached workout data are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Signing out removes the stored sign-in tokens from the device. Uninstalling the app removes the app's private storage, but on iOS items saved in the Keychain can remain after an uninstall, so sign out before uninstalling to remove your sign-in tokens from the device.

### 9.7 Retention & Deletion

We retain this data for as long as your membership account with your gym is active and as required by your gym's and our legal and accounting record-keeping obligations. Your gym, as the data controller, determines how long its membership records are kept. You can start a deletion request in the app (Profile → Delete account), which opens our [Account & Data Deletion](/data-deletion/) page, or directly on that page, or by contacting [hello@thing.bh](mailto:hello@thing.bh); you may also ask your gym. After a deletion request, some records may be kept where required by applicable law or for legitimate accounting and legal purposes: invoices, payment and accounting records, attendance and access logs where the gym must keep them, and records needed to establish or defend legal claims.

### 9.8 Children & Age

The member apps are used by members whose accounts are created by their gym, and are not directed at children. Members are registered by their gym; where a member is a minor, the gym obtains the consent of a parent or legal guardian (see section 13).
